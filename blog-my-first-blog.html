Welcome to My Blog

Welcome! This is where I'll document my journey through Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Cybersecurity, and Software Development.

Why AI Security?

AI is becoming an increasingly important part of modern software and critical systems. As these systems become more integrated into our lives, ensuring their safety, reliability, and security becomes essential.

I want to explore how AI systems can be secured, tested, and made more trustworthy.

Why Mechanistic Interpretability?

Modern AI models can behave like black boxes. We can observe their inputs and outputs, but understanding what happens internally is much harder.

Mechanistic interpretability aims to investigate the internal workings of neural networks and understand how they process information.

I find this particularly interesting because understanding how models work is an important step towards building safer and more reliable AI systems.
