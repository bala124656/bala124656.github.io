01 / ABOUT

A little about me.

I've always been interested in a lot of different things, especially technology. I like understanding how things work, taking them apart conceptually and figuring out how to build or improve them.

BACKGROUND

My story

My interest in programming started around Grade 8, and grew through Grades 9 and 10. I began by writing Lua scripts for Roblox, then experimented with scripts for older games I played on PC, including the Game Boy Advance version of Pokémon FireRed. Those early experiments made programming feel less like a school subject and more like a way to explore the things I enjoyed.

INTERESTS

What keeps me curious

I'm interested in a broad mix of fields, from cybersecurity and biotechnology to software and machine learning. My main focus is AI security: understanding how models behave, finding weaknesses and working out how to make them more robust.

MAIN DIRECTION

AI security, from the inside out.

I'm especially interested in mechanistic interpretability: studying the internal representations and computations of neural networks to better understand why models behave the way they do. I want to use that understanding to inspect models, identify risks and help harden them.
